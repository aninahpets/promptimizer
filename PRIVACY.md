# Promptimizer — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

Promptimizer is a Chrome extension that turns a rough idea into a polished AI
prompt. It is built to be private by design.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Promptimizer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any
personal or user data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no
server operated by us.

## How your data is handled

- **What you type** (your idea, your tuning choices) is processed **entirely on
  your device** by Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano). It is never sent to us or
  to any third party.
- **Settings and in-progress state** (your provider choice, the four tuning
  levers, and the current step of the flow so the popup can resume) are stored
  **locally in your browser** using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never
  leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the extension.
- **No network requests carry your data.** The only external request the
  extension makes is loading display fonts from Google Fonts' CDN
  (`fonts.googleapis.com` / `fonts.gstatic.com`). As with any web font, Google
  may record the request (including your IP address) to serve the font; no
  extension data or anything you type is included in that request.

## Permissions

- **`storage`** — used only to save the settings and resumable state described
  above, on your device.

The extension requests no host permissions and runs no remote code.

## Children's privacy

Promptimizer is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
